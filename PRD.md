# CareGuide Guam — Demo PRD

## Purpose

Give Mrs. Therese a tangible way to react to a possible caregiver-support platform before committing to a full build.

## Primary audience

Family caregivers on Guam, especially people looking for understandable next steps while supporting an older adult or a person with a disability.

## Demo goals

1. Show that a caregiver can ask a plain-language question and get a useful, calm response.
2. Make source transparency and local resources part of the core experience.
3. Demonstrate that nonprofit partners can own, review, and approve the content.
4. Make uncertainty safe: the system must admit when no trusted answer exists.

## Included

- Curated question matching
- Three-question caregiver assessment and personalized starting plan
- Guidance pathway with immediate actions
- Guam resource directory organized by need, with expandable eligibility guidance
- Urgent-help routing for medical danger, mental-health crisis, and vulnerable-adult safety
- Printable plan and non-transmitting warm-handoff preview
- Source and review metadata
- Helpful/not-helpful feedback
- Content review mockup

## Not included

- Generative AI or external API calls
- Authentication or a real admin backend
- User profiles, case notes, diagnoses, or sensitive health data
- Live inventory or service availability
- Production-approved medical or legal guidance

## Success criteria for the meeting

- Therese can see her original examples reflected in the experience.
- She can identify which partner organizations should own each answer.
- The conversation shifts from “what could an app be?” to concrete priorities, content sources, and pilot scope.
- Therese can compare an answer-first experience with a guided navigation experience without seeing a crowded home screen.
